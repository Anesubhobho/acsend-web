About Ascend

Concrete solutions with a purpose.

Founded in Harare, Ascend CONCRETE has grown into a trusted name across Zimbabwe's construction sector. We specialise in manufacturing high-quality precast and prestress concrete products.

From residential paving to large-scale infrastructure, our products are built around strength, longevity and practical performance while supporting local jobs and sustainable building practices.
